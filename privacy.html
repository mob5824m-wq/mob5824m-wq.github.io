01 Scope & Hosting

This policy covers Sentinel and Lofi Girl, independent Discord bot projects maintained by mob5824 (“Developer,” “we,” or “us”), to the extent an instance is operated by or on behalf of the Developer. It does not replace Discord’s privacy policy or the policies of other services.

Sentinel is self-hostable software. A community that installs and runs its own copy controls that instance’s machine, files, access, backups, and retention. The Developer does not automatically receive records from an independently self-hosted copy and cannot access or delete its local database. If you use a community-hosted copy, ask that instance’s operator how it handles data.

As of October 7, 2026, the public Discord-lofi repository does not include implementation code or technical data-flow documentation. Lofi Girl’s exact processing, hosting, audio provider, and retention therefore cannot be verified from that repository. The Lofi Girl information below is intentionally limited; contact the operator of the live instance for specifics and do not send sensitive information to it.

Website notice: This static site is separate from the Bots. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and stores the selected color theme in your browser’s local storage. GitHub Pages, Google, Discord, and your browser handle their own processing under their respective policies.

02 Information Sentinel Processes

A Sentinel instance processes the information needed for the features its server operator enables. The following categories are stored by the instance on the host machine (or in the storage configured by its operator):

Category Examples Why It Is Needed
Discord identifiers Server, member, moderator, role, channel, message, and ticket-thread IDs. Associate settings and actions with the correct server, member, role, or Discord location.
Moderation records Warnings; punishment reason, duration, start/end time, moderator, roles, and status. Apply timed role actions, restore state after a restart, and provide the configured moderation history.
Tickets Ticket subject, category, opener and staff IDs, status, timestamps, close reason, and channel/thread IDs. Route, manage, and show the ticket queue. The local database stores ticket records; the conversation itself takes place in Discord.
Applications Form answers, form name and questions, applicant and reviewer IDs, decision, note, and timestamps. Send submissions for review and show their status. Answers are stored in the instance’s SQLite database and may also appear in the configured Discord review channel.
Configuration and diagnostics Configured role/channel IDs, rules and reaction-role settings, ticket/application form settings, bot configuration, and runtime logs. Keep enabled features working and help the instance operator administer and troubleshoot the bot. Configuration may contain bot or dashboard credentials and must be kept private.

Application answers, ticket subjects, and moderation reasons are user-provided text. A server may ask users for personal information through a custom form; only provide information you are comfortable sharing with that server’s authorized staff. Server operators should restrict staff and review-channel access and avoid requesting sensitive data they do not need.

03 Information Lofi Girl May Process

Lofi Girl is intended to provide lofi music playback in Discord. If a deployed version connects to voice or accepts playback commands, Discord and the instance may process identifiers and activity such as the server, user, voice/text channel, command or interaction, playback request, and voice-connection state needed for that feature.

Because the public project repository does not publish implementation code or document its audio source as of this policy’s Last Updated date, we cannot confirm whether a particular live version records or stores audio, which external music provider it uses, what technical logs it keeps, or how long any data is retained. This policy makes no blanket claim that a Lofi Girl instance never records, caches, or shares audio or usage data. The operator of the instance is the best source for those details. Do not use it for confidential conversations or submit passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

04 What Is Not Collected

Sentinel is designed to act on Discord commands and interactions, configured server information, reactions, moderation actions, and ticket/application content—not to create a general archive of ordinary server chat. The Bot does not store a full ticket transcript in its SQLite ticket record; ticket messages and attachments remain in the Discord channel or thread and are subject to Discord and server settings.

  • Sentinel does not receive a Discord user’s IP address from the Discord API. Discord, the machine hosting an instance, or a network proxy may process connection data under their own policies.
  • Sentinel does not connect to voice channels as part of its documented moderation and server-management features.
  • These statements apply to Sentinel’s published feature set only; they are not a statement about an independently modified copy or the undocumented Lofi Girl implementation.

05 How Data Is Used

For Sentinel, the instance uses the information above to:

  • Carry out server actions requested by authorized members, such as timed role changes, warnings, reaction roles, and rules acceptance.
  • Keep moderation timers and records available after a restart, and show configured history or dashboard views.
  • Create tickets, route applications to the appropriate staff channel, and show their status to authorized staff.
  • Send the messages configured by the server, which may include staff notifications or direct messages about a moderation action or application decision.

For Lofi Girl, use is limited to the intended music-bot function of handling playback requests and voice-channel operation, but exact processing depends on the deployed version and its operator.

06 Storage & Retention

Sentinel: By default, the running instance stores its SQLite database, configuration, and log on its host machine. The exact file paths depend on the operating system and operator settings. If the operator uses a cloud server, container, backup service, or other host, that provider may also store or back up the files under the operator’s account.

  • Active punishments are retained while they are in progress; completed actions are moved into moderation history.
  • Warnings remain until an authorized moderator clears them.
  • Ticket and application records remain in the instance database until the operator deletes them or removes the relevant data/database. Sentinel does not promise a universal automatic deletion period.
  • Removing the Bot from a Discord server does not, by itself, guarantee deletion of a self-hosted instance’s database, configuration, logs, or backups.

Lofi Girl: No fixed retention period or storage location is documented in the public project repository as of this policy’s Last Updated date. Ask the live instance operator for its data location, retention, and deletion process.

07 Sharing & Third Parties

The Bots communicate with Discord to receive commands or interactions and to perform requested server actions. Depending on configuration, Sentinel sends moderation notifications, application information, or other messages to Discord channels or users. Those messages are then handled by Discord and visible according to Discord and server permissions.

  • Discord: Discord processes account, server, message, interaction, and (for a voice bot) voice-connection information under its own privacy terms.
  • Instance operator and host: The person running a self-hosted Sentinel instance can access its local database, configuration, and logs. A hosting or backup provider may store those files for that operator.
  • Optional DuckDNS integration: If an operator configures Sentinel to update a DuckDNS address, the instance sends the configured domain, DuckDNS account token, and the connection’s public IP information to DuckDNS. This integration is optional and controlled by the operator.
  • Lofi Girl audio provider: The provider, if any, is not documented in the public project repository as of this policy’s Last Updated date. The live instance operator should identify any provider and its applicable privacy terms.

The Developer does not sell personal information. Data held by independently hosted copies is controlled by their operators and is not automatically shared with the Developer.

08 Access, Deletion & Choices

  • Self-hosted Sentinel: Contact the server or instance operator to request access, correction, or deletion. They control the database and backups; the Developer cannot retrieve or erase those records for you.
  • Developer-operated instance: Email mob5824@duck.com with the Bot name, Discord user or server ID, and a description of the request. Do not include passwords or other secrets. We will handle requests for data we control in accordance with applicable law.
  • Lofi Girl: For a live instance, contact its operator about what is stored and how to delete it; exact procedures depend on that deployment.
  • Discord: Discord independently controls data on its platform. Use Discord’s account and privacy tools for requests about Discord-held information.

Users can stop interacting with a Bot or ask an administrator to remove it. That action does not necessarily delete historical records held by the instance operator or Discord.

09 Security

Sentinel includes access checks for staff actions and an authenticated dashboard that is configured to listen on the local machine by default. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee security. The operator is responsible for restricting access to the host, database, configuration, credentials, dashboard, and backups; using HTTPS and a firewall for any remote dashboard access; and rotating credentials if they are exposed.

If you use an instance operated by someone else, its operator and infrastructure provider may be able to access its data. Do not submit information you would not want that operator or authorized staff to see.

10 Children’s Privacy

The Bots are not intended for anyone below Discord’s minimum age requirement or below a higher minimum age that applies in their location. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. Server operators must consider the ages of their members and should not collect information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe information was submitted inappropriately, contact the instance operator or, for an instance we operate, email us.
