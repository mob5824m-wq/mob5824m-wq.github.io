Store behavior

North Active makes browsing easier. The retailer handles checkout.

This project focuses on the browsing experience: shoppers can search and filter the hosted catalog, open product pages, and keep a local bag. Product data and images come from Athleta Canada; final availability and checkout are handled on that retailer's site. North Active does not process orders or payments.

What shoppers can do

  • Browse a premium storefront landing page
  • Filter the hosted catalog quickly
  • Open direct product pages for every item
  • Continue to the partner site for checkout

What powers it

  • Hosted catalog file from your branch
  • Shared storefront styling and logo system
  • Static product pages under /na/<slug>/
  • Optional backend support for future feeds

North Active is an independent storefront experiment. Product details, availability, and checkout are provided by the retailer.
