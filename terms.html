02 The Bots

Sentinel is a Discord server-management bot. Depending on its version and configuration, it can provide:

  • Timed moderation roles, pardons, warnings, and moderation history.
  • Published rules and reaction-role menus.
  • Member ticket and application workflows, with staff review tools.
  • An authenticated, server-side dashboard for managing supported features.

Lofi Girl is an independent Discord bot project intended to provide lofi music playback in Discord voice channels. Available commands, music sources, and playback features depend on the version and operator of the instance. The name is used to identify this independent project; unless expressly stated otherwise, it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Lofi Girl, ChilledCow, any music artist or rights holder, or Discord.
