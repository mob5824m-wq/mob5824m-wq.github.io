02 The Software

Depending on version and configuration, Lofi can play supported YouTube broadcasts, HTTP(S) radio streams such as SomaFM channels, files from a local music folder, and tracks generated on the host by Studio Lofi. It may provide slash-command controls, per-server settings, playback announcements, autostart, idle disconnect, and a dashboard.

Audio sources and features may change or be unavailable. The bot relays audio to a Discord voice channel; it does not grant a license to any recording, broadcast, composition, platform, or other content.

04 Operator Responsibilities

The person or organization running a Lofi instance is responsible for its configuration, security, and use. Operators must:

  • Use a Discord bot token they are authorized to use, keep it secret, and grant only permissions needed for the chosen features.
  • Secure the host, configuration, local database, dashboard key, logs, and backups. The dashboard listens on the local machine by default; operators who enable remote access are responsible for HTTPS, firewall rules, host restrictions, and credential security.
  • Choose lawful audio sources, make sure the instance and its use comply with applicable copyright, public-performance, broadcast, and platform rules, and respond to rights-holder or platform notices.
  • Tell server members what the bot does, who can use its controls, and what playback history the instance keeps.
  • Stop playback or remove a source if the operator does not have permission to use it.
